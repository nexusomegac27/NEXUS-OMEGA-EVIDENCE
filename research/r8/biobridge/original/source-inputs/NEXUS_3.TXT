### 3. Dobzhansky-Muller Model — Systems Interpretation

In evolutionary genetics the Dobzhansky-Muller model explains hybrid incompatibility: two lineages accumulate independent mutations that are neutral (or beneficial) in their own backgrounds but deleterious when combined.

**Mapping to BioBridge dual work-lanes X / Y**:

- Lane X and Lane Y may each evolve schema or encoding changes inside \(D\).
- As long as each change remains compatible with the shared Kernel \(K\), the lanes remain interoperable.
- When a change in X is incompatible with a change in Y (both still individually compatible with \(K\)), the merge produces a hybrid incompatibility.
- Correct systems behaviour: non-silent escalation to HOLD/`?` (F21). Silent merge would be the analogue of producing a sterile or inviable hybrid without detection.

**Scientific acceptance criterion**:  
Any independent modification pair that lies outside the Compatibility Kernel must evaluate to HOLD/`?` or FAIL/`!`. The original order correctly identifies this as a critical fixture.
