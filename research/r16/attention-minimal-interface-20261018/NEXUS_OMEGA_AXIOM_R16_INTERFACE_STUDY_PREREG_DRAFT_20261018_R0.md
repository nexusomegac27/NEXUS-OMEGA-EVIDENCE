# NEXUS R16 operator interface study — preregistration DRAFT, not run

Goal: test whether task-focused minimal ledger B reduces material C1/source interpretation errors vs complete static ledger A. Both source-identical, equally accessible.

Design: consented randomized two-period crossover AB/BA, matched tasks; predeclare sample/power from separate pilot, fixed questions, frozen blinded (if possible) answer oracle, excluded data/missing outcomes and corrections. Do not use the actual operator as implicit participant and do not gather GPS or private health signals.

Primary: incorrect provenance/source-rank/HOLD answers; separately track critical false-VERIFIED and authority-confusion counts and denominators.
Secondary: source retrieval time, resumption latency, context switches and NASA-TLX subjective workload.
Null: minimal mode equal or worse; worse critical misclassification fails regardless of speed. Negative controls: hidden HOLD, wrong source pin, invalid resume capsule, auto scrolling. Comparator A never omits content available in B and B never omits material caveats. All designs reversible and read-only.

Explore B vs map C and C vs Resume-Capsule D in separately preregistered studies. Any causal or generalizable claim requires actual approved data and independent review.
