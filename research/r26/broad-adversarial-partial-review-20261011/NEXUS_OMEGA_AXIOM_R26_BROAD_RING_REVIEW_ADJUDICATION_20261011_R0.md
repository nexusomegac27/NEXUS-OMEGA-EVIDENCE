## 2. New fundamental result: non-isolated amplitudes for both naive ReLU models

For fixed W, inhibition gain g, zero additive input and zero noise, with either P_MAX(r)=max_j(r_j) or P_SUM(r)=sum_j(r_j), define F(r)=ReLU(Wr-gP(r)1). ReLU, linear mixing and either penalty operator are positively homogeneous, so for every alpha>0:

F(alpha*r) = alpha*F(r).

This follows algebraically and is illustrated independently with deterministic exact rational two-unit arithmetic. If any nonzero fixed point r* exists, then every alpha*r* is a fixed point too. An **individual** nonzero equilibrium therefore cannot be isolated asymptotically stable. At differentiable fixed points, the radial Jacobian eigenvalue is 1 in discrete time.

This does not prove a specific fixed point exists; does not refute single-bump shape stability or Lyapunov stability; does not decide MAX vs SUM; and does not equate radial amplitude freedom with angular phase freedom. The theorem is limited to ReLU, no bias, no external input, no noise. Saturation, tonic input, threshold/bias and nonlinear normalization may change the conclusion. Weight normalization alone does not necessarily break positive homogeneity. Do not infer the same theorem for the actual Noorman et al. model with nonzero feedforward term.
