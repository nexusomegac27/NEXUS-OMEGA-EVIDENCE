## Operator's scientific law
The causal-intellectual THREAD remains reconstructable as a *labelled possible structural context* even if an expected fragment is missing, seemingly missing, contradictory, private or never observed. Do not lose the parent goal, previously verified segments, competing explanatory hypotheses or next bounded research action merely because one link is unverified.

**WITHOUT VALIDATION** means EXACTLY: we may preserve the coherent surrounding structure and candidate missing-node roles; we may NOT claim that an unobserved event happened, invent original bytes or SHA256, silently close a needed edge, deduce consent, authorization, device execution, proof, independent expert agreement or production status from that structure. Coherence and factual closure are distinct outputs. The law is a normative research continuity constraint, not an empirically validated truth-preserving theorem of all causal systems.

## Formal sketch (design, not a completed causal proof)
Let N be named versioned source/claim/action nodes, E_v edges whose specific relationship has actual bounded evidence, and E_h candidate explanatory/gap edges with exact uncertainty labels. The *context graph* G_c=(N,E_v UNION E_h) can retain a hypothetical route from ancestor to target. The *supported evidence path* G_v=(N_verified,E_v) may NOT close whenever it crosses unverified nodes or edges. Existence of path in G_c DOES NOT entail existence of a verified path in G_v, nor causal identification.
An observed source digest confirms bytes, not historical causation; a verified link is never automatically valid for other links, scientific hypothesis or privileged authorization. No normative rule can create an authenticated edge.

## Required node/gap classes
- ORIGINAL_VERIFIED: actual original bytes + digest + source scope + witness, limited to byte integrity.
- OBSERVED_RUNTIME: exact command, binary, logs and independent witness, scoped by implementation.
- DEVICE_OBSERVED: actual on-device source-bound evidence, not host-reported product label.
- REPORTED_ONLY: source reports event, but its primary executable/data original is unavailable.
- NOT_IN_CUSTODY: named original not received in this trusted surface, not proven globally absent.
- NOT_FOUND_BOUNDED: a bounded authorized source search checked exact locations and did not find it, not global nonexistence.
- ACCESS_RESTRICTED: private/original access is denied or unauthorized; DO NOT bypass or publicize original hashes.
- CONFLICTED: two source-bound claims/receipts disagree; preserve both originals, neither silently wins.
- HYPOTHETICAL_BRIDGE: explicit reconstructed potential intermediate, with alternatives, conditions and falsifier. This is never source evidence.
- REFUTED_BY_EVIDENCE: candidate falsified by an identified negative with original replay scope.
- UNKNOWN/NOT_RUN: no testimony of execution and no justified assertion of absence.
A source can have multiple aspects. "not in this upload" is NOT equivalent to "does not exist"; "private" is NOT equivalent to "missing". Distinguish source status from execution, permission and scientific claim status.
