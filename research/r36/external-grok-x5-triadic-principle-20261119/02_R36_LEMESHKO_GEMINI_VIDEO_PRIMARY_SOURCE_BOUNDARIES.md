## R36 transfer
The direct lesson is a NONCOMPENSATORY FOUR-LAYER CLAIM LEDGER:
1. FORMAL_STATEMENT_AND_SOURCE: exact proposition, assumptions, proof file and version.
2. KERNEL_EXECUTION: genuine pinned toolchain formal proof check and axiom/statement adequacy (OpenAI external proof claim is not R33 Lean success).
3. NUMERICAL_EMPIRICAL: calibrated experimental references, error and uncertainty, heldout controls.
4. INTERPRETATION_AND_ACTION: what the result means for the actual OPPO/BodyTwin sensor/clock model; reviewer limitations and trust gates.
A mathematical proof does not validate a sensor; a sensor correlation does not prove a kinematic identity; a compelling video or imported abstract is candidate information only.
