## Mandatory research protocol
For EACH material scientific assertion log checked_at, original final URL/redirect/status, publisher and pinned release/commit/DOI, methods *actually read*, actual source bytes SHA if physically obtained or NOT_OBTAINED, licensed use scope, correction/retraction/citation and a second NON-REDUNDANT original scientific contrary path. A real domain does not automatically grant raw-data provenance Q4. A self-reported SHA does not grant AXIOM Q4 nor a distinct Q5 independent actor.

## Four epistemic comparisons the expert must keep separate
1. **Discovery**: finding candidate fault; not proof reachable/exploitable.
2. **Reproduction**: actual offline negative, exact code, source, run+exit; not proof fixed.
3. **Repair**: source-exact patch + positive/negative replay and rollback; not production ready.
4. **Authority**: signing over exact bytes plus issuer/actor role/expiry/revocation + human go/no-go; not scientific truth.
The combination must never be flattened into a "PASS" dashboard.
