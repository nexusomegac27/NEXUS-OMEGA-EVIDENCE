# R44-4 verified external research roots for experiment-depth and efficiency

These are **methodological sources**, not evidence that NEXUS BodyTwin is functioning or that E27–E34 have executed studies. Every material future claim must go through VSRD-C1 original source and a truly nonredundant independent route.

1. Princeton **ScienceAgentBench** `https://hal.cs.princeton.edu/scienceagentbench` (original leaderboard): distinguishes VERIFIED reexecuted scientific research result and actual reported API dollars. Method lesson: test-grounded output plus cost; do not derive NEXUS numeric gain.
2. **FIRE-Bench: Evaluating Agents on the Rediscovery of Scientific Insights** original `https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02905`: full research cycle, rediscovery with empirically verifiable conclusions. Method lesson: an agent summary of another document is NOT equivalent to research rediscovery or experimental execution.
3. Stanford **AI Index 2026—Science** `https://hai.stanford.edu/assets/files/ai_index_report_2026.pdf`: references agent evaluation including per-problem cost and 2400+ cross-domain tasks. We reference public summary, NOT a newly independent raw PDF method audit.
4. NIST **AI RMF MEASURE** `https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/measure/`: uncertainty, documentation, repeatable TEVV and explicit missing metrics, not a product certification.
5. NASA software engineering **Entrance/Exit criteria** `https://swehb.nasa.gov/pages/viewpage.action?navigatingVersions=true&pageId=16451056`: entering a design/review stage distinct from satisfying documented verification criteria.
6. W3C **PROV-DM** `https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/`: source entity / activity / agent and derivation relations; NELFP design influence, not yet conformance certified.
7. Official DSSE protocol `https://github.com/dsse-lf/dsse/blob/master/protocol.md`: signed bytes PAE with trustworthy verifier; does not itself authenticate arbitrary operator role without trusted registry.
8. Official UCI #341 `https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/341/smartphone%2Bbased%2Brecognition%2Bof%2Bhuman%2Bactivities%2Band%2Bpostural%2Btransitions`: original single-smartphone data candidate with offchain-reported 79,596,192B source ZIP SHA 4ac4ae06…, not AXIOM direct Q4 in these uploads.
9. FDA/OSEL primary `https://github.com/dbp-osel/wearables-for-gait-synchronized-smartphone-imu-and-walkway-data`: true two different-body-site phone example with independent reference, source conflict 370 vs374 preserved.
10. OpenSSF `https://oss-vulnerability-guide.openssf.org/maintainer-guide.html`: coordinated disclosure; never demand unsafe public raw exploit to meet proof-depth goals.

**Independent source sampling rule**: Two pages from one content root are not two independent scientific methods. DOI/paper and cloned press release are not a corroborating experiment. Official source downloaded through two transports verifies byte parity but not independent causal source. Absence of source/license → explicitly QX and source gap.
