hypothesis. World Model Science is treated as contemporary research with its stated limitations.
CIRIS remains an open project candidate with independent external evaluation as a gap.

Independent search did not establish authoritative public objects for the exact "Flamewalker Agentic
Protocol" or "HE2-Net" claims supplied in the synthesis. Both remain SOURCE_GAP.
