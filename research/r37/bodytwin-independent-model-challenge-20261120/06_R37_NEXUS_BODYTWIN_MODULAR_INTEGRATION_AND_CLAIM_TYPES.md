## Physics and observability
A plausible model is accelerometer specific-force observation f_s = R^T(a-g)+b_a+n_a; gyro omega_m = omega_true+b_g+n_g, with signs/frames/calibration explicitly pinned. The actual Android event sensor type may already be calibrated by device HAL, so describe that boundary. The yaw and global translation of an unconstrained free IMU cannot generally be fixed from accel/gyro alone. Full body pose is nonidentifiable with one unmounted phone, even when a learned model generates point estimates. Two unknown phone-to-body placements do not imply full human observability. Correct NIS/NEES selection and non-Gaussian uncertainty must be considered only where independent truth is available.
