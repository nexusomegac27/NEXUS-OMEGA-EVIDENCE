# R45 independent source prevalidation (2026-11-25)
- Dissipation-Reliability Tradeoff for Stochastic CMOS Bits in Series, arXiv:2603.04658 (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04658. Abstract: model-specific voltage and chain-length scaling; no NEXUS bit-flip rate.
- Soft Error Rate Evaluation and Silent Data Corruption Detection for Reliable TFHE Computations on GPUs, IEEE https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11614168 (2026), 7.80 FIT for decrypted 32-bit TFHE adder output extrapolated from accelerated proton irradiation. Not generic GPU/CPU/device rate. DRAM trends conditional on technology, ECC and exposure; exact proposed DRAM article not primary-full-text checked. Counterpoint 10nm SRAM results https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10969837. 
- Google Cores that don't count https://research.google/pubs/cores-that-dont-count/ (2021); Meta Silent Data Corruptions at Scale https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11245; Chatzopoulos et al Veritas HPCA 2025 https://doi.org/10.1109/HPCA61900.2025.00012. Fleet prevalence roughly one per thousand CPUs/devices in certain reports is not per operation or FIT; no causal assignment to thermal or radiation alone. 
- Bartl, Kupper, Lux, Papapantoleon, Eckstein, Marginal and Dependence Uncertainty: Bounds, Optimal Transport, and Sharpness, https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.00641 (2017), SIAM J Control Optim 2022 https://doi.org/10.1137/21M144709X. Pointwise sharpness in specified uncertainty classes with partial dependence information; not a general physical noise law. Classical exact marginal bounds remain sharp for unrestricted event dependence; their counterexample concerns improved bounds in constrained classes. R45 event interval formula is independently derived under rectangular marginal uncertainty, not the entire paper's theorem.
- Faruk Alpay, Baris Basaran, Self-Verifying Measurement Records: Hash-Linked Evidence Graphs for Hardware Benchmarking, arXiv:2606.27934v1 26 June 2026 https://arxiv.org/html/2606.27934v1. Sections 3,5,10,11: measured residual maximum times margin 3; precision/size/device-specific, not directly FIT. Paper's calibrated detection proposition conditional on sufficiently observable deviations; abstract claim must not become blanket 2^-k for every nonzero error. Finite measured maximum not guaranteed future bound. Reported cross-device results limited to studied platform. Preprint, ancillary code not independently replicated. No stress or device action in R45. 
- Optimeas Open Streaming Format 5 vendor documentation https://docs.optimeas.com/en/docs/software/data_formats/osf/references/osf5/ and https://docs.optimeas.com/docs/software/data_formats/osf/references/osf5_integrity/ (indexed source; second full fetch failed). Optional CRC32C and signed level Ed25519/X509, vendor profile not universal standard or Open Science Framework. CRC detects many accidental errors, does not prove intent; ECC correction conditional on code and fault model; cryptographic integrity is exact, signatures verify origin under trusted keys, no truth guarantee.
- RFC9162 https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9162.html official normative byte encoding; JCGM 100:2008 https://www.bipm.org/en/doi/10.59161/jcgm100-2008e for measurement uncertainty.
