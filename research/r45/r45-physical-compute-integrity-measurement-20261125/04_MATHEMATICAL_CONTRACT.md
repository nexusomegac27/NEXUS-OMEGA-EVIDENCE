# R45 mathematical contract (design, C1)
For true event A_i and recorded B_i, if P(A_i xor B_i)<=epsilon_i then |P(A_i)-P(B_i)|<=epsilon_i. For simultaneous sampling intervals [L_i,U_i] on recorded probabilities and defensible upper error bounds epsilon_i, true p_i in [max(0,L_i-epsilon_i),min(1,U_i+epsilon_i)]. Joint outer identification interval [max(0,sum l_i-(d-1)),min u_i]; sharp for rectangular marginal sets and otherwise unrestricted dependence, not necessarily with extra constraints. Confidence level on estimated bounds distinct from identification width. If error bound unknown, do not substitute FIT; use unknown/worst-case as appropriate. For observed per-trial joint B use |P(all A)-P(all B)|<=P(union mismatches)<=min(1,sum epsilon_i), or tighter independently evidenced joint label-error rate. Four nominal .95 with illustrative ±.01 gives [.76,.96], exact marginals [.80,.95]. 
Serial chain union bound P(any fault)<=min(1,sum p_i) without independence; product only justified by independence or conditional model. FIT lambda/1e9 hours; hypothetical Poisson constant hazard P>=1 in t =1-exp(-lambda*t/1e9); separate device prevalence, workload exposure, severity, propagation. Zero failures in n iid trials one-sided 95% upper 1-.05^(1/n); clustering/time dependence invalidates iid. 
Exact Freivalds for fixed AB!=C over a field with independent uniform binary probe, reliable arithmetic, per-probe miss<=1/2; repeat k independent gives <=2^-k. With tolerance tau>0, scalar D=.5*tau produces residual <=tau for EVERY binary probe. No k repairs this blind zone. With exact arithmetic and some |D_ij|>2*tau, conditioning on other bits gives at most one of two values within [-tau,tau], hence <=1/2 per probe for infinity norm; if verifier rounding perturbation bounded eta, sufficient |D_ij|>2*(tau+eta). No blanket probabilistic guarantee for other normalized residuals, arbitrary faults, adaptive producers or faulty verifier. Define norm and detectable effect class first, independently measure numerical noise floor and finite-sample uncertainty, freeze threshold, test held-out negatives and positive controls. A measured floor is not a hardware SDC rate, k not derived from floor alone. 
Evidence layers: raw capture + timestamp/reference uncertainty; exact canonical bytes and CRC; independent numerical oracle/guard; SHA256, signature with trusted key, external checkpoint; authorization gate exact. Pre-seal corrupted value may hash/sign correctly. Post-seal change mismatches exact digest. Both accidental/malicious can break same hash; do not attribute from checksum. Numerical acceptance never overrides identity, integrity, STOP or scope. Missing controls -> UNKNOWN/HOLD lane.
