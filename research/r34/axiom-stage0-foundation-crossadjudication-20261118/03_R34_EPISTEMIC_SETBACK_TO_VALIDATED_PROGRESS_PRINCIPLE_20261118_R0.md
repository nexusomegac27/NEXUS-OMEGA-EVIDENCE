## 1. Operator principle

"Gerade aus aufgedeckten Rueckschlaegen und Fehlannahmen kann echte Entwicklung entstehen: Nicht durch das Wegerklaeren des Irrtums, sondern durch Motivation zur offenen Pruefung, Ursachenfindung, Korrektur und unabhaengigen Selbstkontrolle."

Operational translation: a failed hypothesis, security counterexample or invalid receipt is potentially high-value information. It is NOT automatically a success. Only a documented increase in explanatory or predictive performance that survives a new independent test constitutes verified method progress.

The following separate objects MUST NOT be conflated:
- FAILED_TEST is a new observation, not automatically PROVEN_MODEL_FALSE.
- REPORTED_DEFECT is a hypothesis until original bytes and executable witness support it.
- CONFIRMED_COUNTEREXAMPLE rebuts the specified proposition within its stated preconditions; it does not automatically rebut other propositions.
- PATCH_PRESENT is not FIX_VALIDATED.
- FIX_VALIDATED_OFFLINE is not WWW_DEPLOYED, EMPIRICAL_BODYTWIN_VALIDATED or SCIENTIFIC_TRUTH.
- RESEARCHER_MOTIVATION is a process and culture goal, not empirical proof or a claim about AI consciousness.

## 2. Required falsification-to-improvement loop

G0 PRESERVE: Record immutable baseline (commit, file SHA256, input bytes, version, trust assumptions, claim ceiling), including failed fixture. Never erase or rewrite negative evidence to obtain a green status.
G1 PREDICT: State original intended claim, null/alternative hypotheses and exact falsifiable boundary before the test.
G2 OBSERVE: Collect original machine evidence, measurement uncertainty and source-class (independent replay, producer report, design-only, missing original), including failure or inconclusive result.
G3 ADJUDICATE: Classify as CONFIRMED_COUNTEREXAMPLE / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE / NOT_REPRODUCED / EXPECTED_NEGATIVE / IMPLEMENTATION_DEFECT / MODEL_SCOPE_LIMIT / UNKNOWN. State why competing explanations were rejected.
G4 LEARN: Explain the minimum causal failure mechanism and the smallest change to model, code, tests or scope; record what remains unchanged and unknown.
G5 REPAIR: Use a scoped and authorized Cursor order, quarantine unsafe inputs and create an additional regression fixture from the observed failure. Avoid redundant fixture-only work at saturation.
G6 REPLAY: Distinct reviewer reruns exact versions and negative/positive controls. A passing source digest or a pretty test count alone is insufficient.
G7 PROMOTE_OR_HOLD: Only under the existing claim, owner, privacy, source and release gates. If any material gate remains open, publish only source-safe descriptive research with HOLD clearly visible. A HOLD is a precise work queue, not a moral judgement.
G8 RETAIN: Add the validated delta, failed earlier version, decision rationale and rollback path to the append-only causal ledger. Do not erase the mistaken predecessor.

## 3. Observable process indicators (not truth metrics)

Track:
A. Fraction of confirmed failure cases converted into source-bound reproducible regression fixtures.
B. Fraction of declared fixes independently replayed with original source and toolchain hashes.
C. Rate of recurrence of the same confirmed defect after release.
D. Percentage of claims with explicit uncertainty, alternative explanation and falsification criterion.
E. Time from independent defect evidence to source-bound adjudication, without rewarding unsafe haste.

Never optimize solely for the number of failures, PASS counts, fewer HOLDs, apparent autonomy, or speed. A negative result may be the correct terminal result.
