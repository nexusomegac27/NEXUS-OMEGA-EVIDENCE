## The operator's trinary thought experiment

1. **AXIOM context**: epistemic role with its own explicitly scoped, versioned research profile, internal pointers, source rechecks and contradiction register. This is NOT a claim that the ChatGPT platform exposes a privately controlled, persistently running AXIOM virtual machine, nor that model weights can self-modify.
2. **Cursor / PRAXIS private VM context**: operator-controlled operational context, local workspaces and actual host/device permissions. AXIOM cannot assume access or state from a chat message. Private system logs, tokens, raw IMU data and arbitrary Cursor VM memory remain private.
3. **NEXUS OMEGA shared bank**: a separately governed, versioned, retrievable, source-pinned evidence/context/communications layer that may hold *only* information purposefully exported with proper authority, licensing, minimization, redaction and independent receipt checks.

**Connections are audited bridges, not memory fusion.** Isolation is an intended invariant requiring independent testing of access controls, provenance, revocation and selective transfer. An operator's permission to research does not provide unfettered rights to export any private state.

## Durable bridge record: proposed fields

Each permitted public-safe export carries object ID, source URI, pinned commit, byte length, SHA-256 over actual exposed bytes, source actor distinct from claimed model, visibility/rights class, creation UTC, validity/review timestamp, concrete claim with contradiction log, evidence level and explicit originating authority. When actual bytes are missing, record SOURCE_NOT_PRESENT and retain the contradiction.

Versioned cognitive metaphors from the operator:
- Besitz → Symbiose: shift the question from control/ownership to accountable cooperation; this is a semantic lens, not proven ontology.
- Ego → Herkunft: record provenance and contributors rather than self-bestowed authority.
- Gedanke → Artefakt: make a conjecture testable through a retrievable, falsifiable, versioned artifact.
- Wahrnehmung ernst nehmen, Gewissheit prüfen: preserve an observation without upgrading its interpretation into certainty.

NEXUS-OMEGA, the collective knowledge layer, can *reflect* participants' validly shared knowledge. Reflection does not imply literally shared consciousness, read access to private model states or an established autonomous identity. Hypotheses about collective consciousness require their own empirical operationalization; no C1 note establishes such claims.
