## Individual actor creative method
A research actor proposes ≥3 genuinely competing testable hypotheses and ≥2 source routes, then chooses evidence by falsification value, feasibility, independence, cost and legal rights; records forecast, result, error, negative, honest abstention and next strategy revision. Three bounded iterations max without new evidence or authorization. Persona independence never substitutes for separately registered actor trust. No repeated source summary counts as source gap closure.
