## 1. Source-specific independent mathematical adjudication — essential errata
**A. Local is not global:** A nonsingular regular FIM is at most a criterion for local identification under stated regularity, parametrization and likelihood. It does NOT establish global identifiability, physical observability or practical calibration; profile likelihood, global symmetries and actual data must be evaluated. Source primary Rothenberg (1971) https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~dbackus/Identification/id/Rothenberg_id_Econometrica_71.pdf ; modern structural/practical source https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S245231002100007X . NEXUS kinematic state may have gauge symmetries and only a *target functional* may be identifiable.
**B. Target-specific abstention, not blanket refusal:** A rank-1 likelihood matrix for theta=(theta1,theta2), y=theta1+theta2+noise, has full null along theta1-theta2 yet theta1+theta2 IS identifiable. Reject ungrounded individual-state estimates and allow valid *identified functional* with uncertainty/provenance. ABSTAIN for target-specific failure/risk, not merely because *some unrelated* nuisance parameter lacks identification.
**C. Identical placement does not guarantee zero information gain:** AXIOM independently executed a small NumPy scalar Gaussian counterexample with equal sensor geometry and equal marginal variance 1: I(phone A)=1; I(A,B)=2 for independent noise rho=0, 4/3 for rho=.5 and 2/(1+.9)=1.05263 for rho=.9. Real source correlation and shared timing must be quantified. The Grok text's "identical mounting + identical covariance = no incremental information" is **FALSE without a fully shared/no-independent-innovation condition**. The second sensor can reduce uncertainty even at identical location.
**D. Fisher absolute eigenvalue / threshold scaling:** For measurement y~N(theta,1), I(theta)=1 under one unit; reparameterize phi=1e6 theta and I(phi)=1e-12, so `eps_rank=1e-8` would *incorrectly label the same experiment non-identifiable*. The claimed `kappa_crit=1e8`, `tau_crit=.85`, `weak_frac=.05` are uncalibrated candidate knobs, not recognized physical standards. Preregister normalized/whitened dimensionless physical parameter scales and sensitivity intervals; do not use global absolute numeric epsilon without unit specification and scale/matrix uncertainty.
**E. Null-eigenvalue ratio metric is defective:** Suggested `alpha=sum(lambda_i where lambda_i<eps)/trace(I)` evaluates zero for **exactly zero eigenvalues**, missing real rank deficiency. Instead output rank, nullity, min nonzero normalized eigenvalue, sensitivity to epsilon and target projection onto nullspace; where `trace=0` handle fail-closed separately. Eigenvectors and prior ratio also depend on parametrization/scaling.
**F. Classical CRB is conditional:** `Cov(theta_hat)>=I^-1` assumes appropriate regularity, unbiasedness and full rank; biased/prior-based estimator may have smaller variance at cost of bias; ordinary GUM uncertainty is NOT generally `I^-1` and cannot be replaced by an asymptotic CRB. Official [BIPM/JCGM 100:2008](https://www.bipm.org/en/doi/10.59161/jcgm100-2008e) and [JCGM 101:2008](https://www.bipm.org/en/doi/10.59161/jcgm101-2008) require a proper measurement model/propagation and covariance of input quantities.
**G. Bayesian additivity has conditions:** Local Hessian of log posterior is sum of prior and likelihood Hessians, and expected Bayesian information may add under assumptions (van Trees inequality), but `Cov_post=(I_prior+I_Fisher)^-1` is exact only in special Gaussian/linear cases or a local Laplace approximation; not a general GUM identity or proof of calibration. The *same prior must appear only once* for both phone likelihoods. A prior-dominated identifiable posterior can be useful for a predictive prior-based estimate, but its output must be labeled `PRIOR_DOMINATED_NOT_MEASUREMENT_IDENTIFIED` and judged by separate loss/risk, not laundered into measurement accuracy. Reference https://web.stanford.edu/class/ee378c/lecture14_annotated.pdf and https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aditya/resources/STAT212aOCT23Lecture9.pdf .
**H. Information delta requires a joint model:** `I(A,B)-I(A)` is a local conditional *likelihood* information difference for the same target/experiment under a consistent joint density; no blanket claim of performance/clinical gain, and independent additive FI is invalid under cross-correlation without correct joint likelihood and nuisance/Schur complement. Oracle/reference inputs in test data are prohibited for deployable phone-only estimates (K07).
**I. Fisher and NIS/NEES:** Sample mean NIS≈1 from historic operator-reported C1 data is not proof of a calibrated UKF; distributional, temporal, model mismatch, multi-device covariance, gating/selection and independent truth checks necessary; NEES requires true state and is not estimated from synthetic reference labels alone.
