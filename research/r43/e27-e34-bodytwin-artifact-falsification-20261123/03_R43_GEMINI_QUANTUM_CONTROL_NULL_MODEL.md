# Gemini's quantum-Galileo analogy — falsifiable narrow methodology, not physics promotion

Source context: operator-provided Gemini remarks about 2026 Quantum Galileo Interferometer, Science Advances 12(36) eaec8045, DOI https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aec8045 . Public primary bibliographic description says interferometer splits matter waves into a supported laboratory-frame branch and a free-falling branch and examines relative quantum phase. This is a genuine quantum-physics experiment, not a digital human twin study.

**Allowed methodological translation**:
1. Explicit comparator arm vs experiment arm; measured observable must be differential, not mere same-system self-confirmation.
2. Define independent calibrations for clock/angle/position and uncertainties, including correlated common modes.
3. Null model H0: apparent benefit from common-mode suppression or better synchronization is absent after controlling for reference precision and clock drift. H1: differential protocol demonstrably improves reproducibility, bias or U95 coverage vs a simpler *classical* baseline on a common independent data set.
4. Blind or preregistered variants: aligned vs randomized reference timing; same device vs independent optical ground truth; mounting disturbance and magnetometer contamination; covariance diagonal vs full; missingness/packet-latency adversaries.
5. In R43 no experiment with quantum hardware, no attempt to infer quantum behavior, gravity, quantum consciousness, or personal physiology from smartphone IMUs. Any such extrapolation = REJECT_UNSUPPORTED.
6. Acceptance only: reproducible classical differential-reference experimental design + original permitted artifacts, null/positive controls, actual uncertainty budget and effect size. “Einstein's equivalence principle verified at quantum scale” is **not** a NEXUS BodyTwin result and cannot lift BODYTWIN_VALIDATED=NO.

Gemini's framing of C1 as an experimental falsification discipline is useful as a heuristic; comparisons to quantum Darwinism, Popper or zero-trust are analogies, not independent evidence and should not substitute any real validation test.
