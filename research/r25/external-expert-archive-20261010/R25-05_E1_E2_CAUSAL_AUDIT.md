## 1. Definitions (binding)

**E1 — Observed risk signals**  
Media-reported internal Meta metrics (Reuters, 26 Aug 2026, Katie Paul) showing:

- large increase in code-change volume (+220 % YoY on internal platforms)
- substantially smaller increase in user-facing features (+36 %)
- elevated major technical/security incidents (~+40 %)
- elevated firefighting time (~+70 %)

under a period of increased AI-agent / coding-tool deployment and organisational pressure toward an “AI-native” model (Project OT scenario planning).

E1 is a **descriptive association**. It does **not** establish that the absence of a provenance ledger was the cause.

**E2 — Prospective ablation / self-stabilisation hypothesis**  
The conjecture that large-scale removal of experienced human lanes (the 60 % scenario) would have produced a measurable collapse of system self-stabilisation, analogous to unit ablation in a ring attractor.  

E2 was **never experimentally realised** at Meta: the second wave was cancelled. It remains a model projection only.

## 3. Confounders and alternative explanations (must be carried)

1. Reorganisation effects, hiring freezes, and performance-management changes independent of agents.
2. Incompatible denominators or population definitions in the reported percentages.
3. Selection / reporting bias in what internal metrics were leaked or highlighted.
4. General increase in system complexity and coupling, not specifically agent-generated code.
5. Measurement of “features shipped” vs. “code changes” may capture different underlying processes.
6. Security-incident classification and detection sensitivity may have changed contemporaneously.

Any causal story that ignores these remains under-determined.

---

## 4. Analogical boundaries

- **Stanford dual-origin** and **Drosophila ring** supply **heuristics**, not formal isomorphisms.
- Organisational lanes are not lineage-restricted progenitors.
- Human engineers are not E-PG units.
- Therefore: biological or attractor analogies may motivate hypotheses; they do not prove organisational theorems.

---

## 5. Falsification conditions (for future work)

- **E1 causal ledger hypothesis is weakened** if controlled agent-deployment studies with and without strong provenance/review chains show no reliable difference in incident rate or auditability after confounders are accounted for.
- **E2 is weakened** if controlled unit-ablation experiments on a ring (or on a software system with clear modular structure) show that 40–60 % removal, with allowed retuning, leaves persistence and correction capability intact.
- **E2 is strengthened** only by pre-registered ablation results that demonstrate clear, monotonic loss of stabilisation metrics.

Until such results exist, both E1-causality and E2 remain outside the set of established claims.
